HOW LEO BROADBAND OPERATES

Direct-to-orbit connectivity delivering fiber-speed latency across Kathmandu via low Earth orbit satellites orbiting at 550 km.

ORBITAL ADVANTAGE

WHY LOW EARTH ORBIT MATTERS

Traditional satellite internet relies on single geostationary satellites orbiting at 35,786 km. This creates severe latency (>600 ms), making real-time voice calls, online video conferences, gaming, and interactive cloud apps frustrating and laggy.

Nepal Space Telecom connects to an orbital mesh of Low Earth Orbit (LEO) satellites at just 550 km altitude — over 65 times closer to Kathmandu. Signal round-trips take only milliseconds, delivering low latency comparable to ground fiber.

Monsoon Resistant

Electronic phased-array beamforming actively alters signal polarization during heavy rainfall to prevent signal degradation during intense Kathmandu monsoon seasons.

Self-Melting & Weatherproof

Integrated thermal heating automatically melts snow, frost, and ice on the dish surface, keeping high-altitude links clear year-round.

Plug & Play Setup

Unpack, plug into power, and point at an unobstructed sky. The automated internal motor handles precise satellite acquisition and tracking within minutes.
