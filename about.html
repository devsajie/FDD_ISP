OUR ORIGIN

HEADQUARTERED IN MAITIDEVI

Nepal Space Telecom Pvt. Ltd. was founded to build resilient, space-based telecommunications infrastructure across Nepal. Operating out of our operations center in Maitidevi, Kathmandu, we manage ground node alignment, network stability, and customer deployments.

Nepal’s challenging terrain and seismic activity frequently disrupt underground fiber cables. By communicating directly with Low Earth Orbit satellites, Nepal Space Telecom provides continuous connectivity that remains unaffected by landslides, road construction, or severe weather.
