SPACE ARCHITECTURE

WHY LOW EARTH ORBIT MATTERS

Traditional satellite internet relies on single geostationary satellites orbiting at 35,786 km. This creates severe latency, making real-time voice calls, gaming, and interactive apps impossible.

Nepal Space Telecom operates a mesh system of Low Earth Orbit (LEO) satellites at just 550 km. Because signal travel distances are dramatically shorter, you get fiber-grade latency directly over the air.
