SPACE ARCHITECTURE

WHY LOW EARTH ORBIT MATTERS

Traditional satellite internet relies on single geostationary satellites orbiting at 35,786 km. This creates severe latency, making real-time voice calls, gaming, and interactive apps impossible.

Nepal Space Telecom operates a mesh system of Low Earth Orbit (LEO) satellites at just 550 km. Because signal travel distances are dramatically shorter, you get fiber-grade latency directly over the air.

GEOSTATIONARY (35,786 km) — 600ms Latency
NEPAL SPACE TELECOM GRID (550 km) — 20ms Latency

ENGINEERED FOR EXTREMES

BUILT FOR NEPALI TERRAIN & WEATHER

Monsoon Resistant

Electronic phased-array beamforming actively alters signal polarization during heavy rainfall to prevent signal degradation during monsoon seasons.

Self-Melting & Weatherproof

Integrated thermal heating automatically melts snow, frost, and ice on the dish surface, keeping links clear year-round.

Plug & Play Setup

Unpack, plug into power, and point at an unobstructed sky. The automated internal motor handles precise satellite tracking within minutes.
